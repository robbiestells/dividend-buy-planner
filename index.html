Weights are normalized, so they don't need to add to 100. A negative distribution change models the slow NAV erosion common in option-income funds.

How the score works

Total return blends three things: where the price sits in its 52-week range (lower is cheaper), whether recent distributions are rising or shrinking, and a sustainability check that discounts yields far above 12%, since those usually come partly out of NAV.

Monthly dividend rewards a higher yield on the price you pay, with a small bonus when the next ex-dividend date lands 1 to 10 days after the buy, so the new shares are paid that month.

Balance favors funds under their target weight. The order is built one share at a time, re-scoring after each share, so a large cash amount spreads across funds instead of piling into one.

This is a rules-based helper, not financial advice. Ex-dividend dates are estimated from each fund's recent pattern; confirm them with your broker.
