Scoring weights and goal tap to adjust

Weights are normalized, so they don't need to add to 100. A negative distribution change models the slow NAV erosion common in option-income funds.

How the score works

Total return blends where the price sits in its 52-week range (lower is cheaper), whether recent distributions are rising or shrinking, and a sustainability check that discounts yields far above 12%, since those usually come partly out of NAV.

Monthly dividend rewards a higher yield on the price you pay, with a small bonus when the next ex-dividend date lands 1 to 10 days after the buy.

Balance favors sectors below their target and, within a sector, funds below their share of it. The order is built one share at a time, re-scoring after each share, so cash spreads out.

Total return counts distributions you entered plus those the planner estimates each time you record a purchase. This is a rules-based helper, not financial advice; ex-dividend dates are estimates, and selling can create taxes.
